# Brand Guidelines

## Identity (verified)
- Name: **eCard**. Wordmark is "eCard", with a lowercase e and capital C.
- Descriptor: "AI visiting card CRM".
- Tagline: "The AI visiting-card CRM. Scan, remember and follow up with every person you meet."
- Hero line: "Every card you collect, scanned into a relationship."
- Logo: `public/brand/ecard-icon.svg`, downloaded from https://ecard.gg/brand/ecard-icon.svg. Don't recolour or stretch it.

## Colour
The brand colours come from the official logo's gradient stops. No other brand colours are published.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| brand-400 | #BA6AFB | Highlights on dark backgrounds |
| brand-600 | #7F19F7 | Primary buttons, links, accents |
| brand-800 | #3202B8 | Gradient end, deep accents |
| ink-950 | #0A1020 | Dark sections, headings |
| ink-500 | #5F6B82 | Secondary text |
| mint-600 | #0F9D77 | Success and checkmarks |
| amber-500 | #F0A020 | Low-confidence and warning states |

## Type
Geist Sans and Geist Mono (through `next/font`). The reference site's font couldn't be identified, so this is a design choice.

## Voice
Direct, practical and warm. Lead with outcomes ("follow up with the whole room"). Use Indian English and ₹ pricing. No hype, no unverifiable superlatives, no invented social proof.
