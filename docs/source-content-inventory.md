## Features (homepage)
- **AI scanning:** you choose Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek or Grok (the FAQ adds Azure and custom endpoints). You can bring your own key. Each field gets a confidence score, and nothing is saved until you review it. Supports a key pool with failover. Addresses are checked against Google Maps.
- **Telegram:** send a photo to the bot and the contact is saved, with Open and Send buttons in the chat. You link the bot with a one-time code from Settings → Telegram.
- **WhatsApp:** AI writes a message in English, Hindi or Marathi and opens it in WhatsApp in one tap.
- **Digital card:** premium templates, QR code, Save-to-contacts, and short links that count clicks with bots filtered out. Example path: /c/rahul. No app needed.
- **Intro emails:** you preview the AI draft, and it sends after 5 seconds from your own SMTP. You can edit or cancel it.
- **Meeting memory:** where you met, who introduced you, voice notes, and a searchable AI summary.
- **Link tracking:** see opens of your card, PDFs and WhatsApp links.
- **Natural-language search:** for example, "Investors I met in Mumbai last month".
- **Events & expos:** group the cards from a trade show and follow up with everyone at once.
- **Clean data:** duplicate merge, blacklist, archive, CSV import, Google Contacts sync.
- **Team & admin:**
  - Roles: owner, admin, manager, member, scan-only guest.
  - Security: 2FA with authenticator codes, recovery codes, trusted devices and login alerts. API keys and SMTP passwords are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Workspaces have an audit log.
  - White-label: logo, colours and custom domain.
  - Webhooks and API, with signed events for each new contact.
  - Compliance checklist: consent, export, deletion.
- **Time-saved calculator:** labelled "Estimates, not promises".
- **Sample scan demo:** three personas (Realtor, Doctor, Startup founder).
